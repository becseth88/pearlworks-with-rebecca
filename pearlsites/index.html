The Pearlsites process

From your Sourcebook
to your website.

We build from the material you have already gathered, with time to review the wording and design together.

  1. 01

    Agree the brief

    We review your Sourcebook and choose what your first website needs to do. We confirm the content, page structure, design direction and booking requirements, then agree the scope, price and timeline.

  2. 02

    Develop the first version

    I turn the agreed material into a simple website draft, using your photographs and other suitable imagery, with writing that helps visitors understand your offering.

  3. 03

    Refine it together

    You review the wording and design. We check that your experience, services and practical details are accurately represented, and make the revisions agreed in your package.

  4. 04

    Connect and test

    I set up the agreed connections for enquiries, bookings, payments and your calendar. We check the website on mobile and desktop, including the route from finding your work to getting in touch.

  5. 05

    Launch and hand over

    Once you approve the website, we publish it to the agreed domain. I show you how to access it and manage the everyday updates included in your setup.
