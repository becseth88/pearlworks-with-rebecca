A few practical things

Getting ready
to get bookable.

Bring the services you are ready to offer, with an idea of the session lengths and prices. We shape the descriptions and a simple bio together. If you would like more time to explore your story, gifts and offering, the five Pearl Sequence Sessions give that work more room.

We agree the setup that suits your offering before the session. This can be a simple Bio Sites presence with a booking route, or a TidyCal booking page. We use the booking, calendar and payment features supported by the chosen setup.

We can connect PayPal and/or Stripe where supported, and choose the payment option for your booking types. In TidyCal, you can connect both providers, with one selected for each booking type. You will need active accounts ready to receive payments.

Your service ideas, intended prices and session lengths, access to your calendar, and an active PayPal or Stripe account for paid bookings. Have your account sign-ins and access to the relevant email ready. Any payment-provider verification needs to be complete so paid bookings can go live during our session.

We agree a focused set of services and the platform beforehand, so we can use the two hours to get the essentials working.

The package covers our two-hour working session and the agreed booking-page setup: your simple bio, service descriptions, booking types, calendar, payment connections, confirmation email and shareable booking link.

We confirm the fee and scope before working together. Platform subscriptions and payment processing fees are separate. A fuller website can be developed through a separately agreed Pearlsites package.
